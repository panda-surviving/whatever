Crypto & Mutual Funds

Different rules from stock exchanges.

Cryptocurrency Markets OPEN
Trades 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — no exchange hours apply.
Pakistan Mutual Funds
NAVs are struck once per business day by each AMC, generally after PSX closes (~4:00 PM PKT) — they don't trade continuously like stocks.

PSX Divergence Screener

52-week-low and RSI-divergence screen across every PSX-listed stock, market-wide.

Walks every PSX symbol's ~1 year of daily price history and flags: stocks near their 52-week low, bullish/bearish RSI divergence anywhere in the market, and divergences that sit within an uptrend or downtrend structure. A full market scan takes several minutes on first run since it covers 700+ symbols one at a time — results are cached so reopening this tab shows your last scan instantly. This is a technical screen, not investment advice.
